Analytic Number Theory:

The study of the distribution of prime numbers. Finding an asymptotic formula for the prime counting function $\pi(x)$. 
$$\pi(x) \sim \int_{0}^{x} \frac{dt}{\log(t)}$$

The error term is related to the Riemann Zeta function.

$$\zeta(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^s}$$
